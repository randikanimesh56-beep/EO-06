Signal rules · streamlined test · EMA 12/26 sets direction (EMA12 above/below EMA26 and price on the same side of EMA12); RSI 14 confirms momentum (BUY above 52 and rising / SELL below 48 and falling); MACD 12/26/9 blocks only when its histogram expands clearly against the EMA direction. The last completed 1M candle only needs to close in the same direction; no engulfing/rejection pattern is required. 15M trend and previous 20 completed 1M candles are context only. A forming candle is never used for entry. Fresh live ticks protect data integrity; the separate economic-news risk guard may block around loaded high/medium-impact events.
